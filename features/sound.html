Audio, MIDI & scores

One concert grand, fetched in one click, and played through your own sound card

The one piano

Salamander Grand Piano V3: a Yamaha C5, fetched once and verified

  • The samples are not inside the installer — that is what keeps the download at 48.8 MiB. Press Download piano · 748 MB in Settings → Audio and the app fetches all 641 FLAC files, one time.
  • The source commit is pinned, every file is checked against its Git blob SHA-1 as it arrives, a valid file already on disk is reused, and a cancelled download resumes where it stopped.
  • Keyflow generates the SFZ mapping for this one library itself — 16 velocity layers, hammer and release samples, pedal noise and string resonance — and loads the instrument as soon as the last file verifies.
  • The recorded samples live in %LocalAppData%\Keyflow\Instruments\SalamanderGrandPiano, and the CC BY 3.0 attribution is written beside them the moment the download completes.

Output & reverb

Pick your audio path, then pick your room

  • OUTPUT & LATENCY offers WASAPI in shared or exclusive mode, an ASIO driver for a studio interface, or waveOut as the fallback, plus the target latency — and a status line that reports the live output and buffer.
  • HALL REVERB is on by default with its own bypass button, and the tail keeps ringing after you release the keys.
  • Beyond the classic hall there is a convolution reverb — partitioned FFT, overlap-save — with a ~2.8 s concert hall and a close soundboard generated inside the app, or your own .wav / .flac impulse response.
  • STRING RESONANCE adds a bank of 64 waveguides — one per string — that rings along while the pedal is down and chokes the moment it lifts. Gain sits next to all of it.

Why the samples are a separate download

748 MB of samples in every installer, in Git and in every ZIP would be paid for by everyone, including the people who never open the Audio page. So the packages stay small, the samples are fetched on request, and the CC BY 3.0 notice travels beside them.

There is no SoundFont import and no external instrument picker: Keyflow reads the opcodes its own SFZ mapping needs, plus WAV in 16/24/32-bit or float32 and FLAC — and nothing else.

Pedals & input

Three pedals, four ways to play a note

Sustain · CC 64

A continuous damper: half-pedalling stretches the release tail instead of holding it outright, repedalling in time catches strings that are still ringing, and the raw CC 64 value of a continuous pedal goes straight to the synthesizer.

Sostenuto · CC 66

Holds only the notes that were already sounding when the pedal went down.

Una corda · CC 67

The soft pedal: lower gain and a gentler timbre.

Computer keyboard

A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play one octave from middle C; hold a key to sustain, release to send Note Off.

On-screen piano

Click or drag over the keys with the full colour, spark and flame response.

MIDI instrument

Velocity, Note On/Off and all three pedals enter the same feedback pipeline; the badge above the keyboard reads MIDI IN · C4 when a note arrives.

Importing scores

MIDI and MusicXML, read the way they were written

  • MIDI formats 0, 1 and 2 — format 2 is a set of independent sequences, so they play one after another.
  • Both time divisions: PPQ, and SMPTE where ticks are absolute time, so the tempo only changes the metronome's speed and never moves a note.
  • Tempo maps, meter, track names, note durations and velocities are read; drum channel 10 is ignored; seek, tempo and A-B loop work on top of all of it.
  • MusicXML in .musicxml, .xml and compressed .mxl (read through META-INF/container.xml): instruments, beats per bar, tempo maps, meter changes mid-song, chords, backup/forward and part names.
  • Broken files are rejected with a reason instead of half-loading, and a MIDI file with an unknown format or an unusable division says so.
The staff layer it feeds

Library

Two lists: what you just played, and the folder you keep

PartHow it works
RECENTThe twelve newest songs with their note and track counts, length, tempo and the preset they were last played at. Click a row to reopen it with those exact values, × to forget it; the Play dialog shows the first five.
LIBRARYCHOOSE FOLDER indexes every MIDI file and MusicXML score under it — three levels deep, up to 500 files — reading each one with the app's own reader to get its notes, tracks, length and tempo.
Tags & searchAdd tags with + TAG… (up to eight per song, trimmed and lower-cased) and remove them by clicking. Search matches titles, file names and tags, and every word has to match.
Folder watcherThe folder is watched, so adding, editing or deleting a file updates the list by itself; RESCAN re-reads everything but reuses the entries that did not change and keeps their tags.
On diskThe index lives in library-index.json and only re-reads files that changed; a corrupt index reads as an empty library instead of an error, and the search box stays hidden until something is indexed.
